1

Get the template

2 minutes

Download it on a laptop or desktop. You'll edit it there. If you're on your phone, send this page to yourself for later.

Download free template ↓ See it live
Unzip it and you'll find one file, index.html. That one file is your whole website.
2

Make it yours

30–45 minutes

Open index.html in a free text editor. VS Code is best; Notepad (Windows) or TextEdit (Mac) also work. Search for ✏️ EDIT and change each spot:

  • Your name: in the title, the top-left logo and the footer
  • Your headline: one line on what you do and for whom
  • Your colour: change --accent to your brand colour
  • Your work: four projects with a name and what you did
  • Your photos: put images in an images folder and swap each placeholder for <img src="images/work.jpg">
  • Your story and numbers: two short paragraphs and three numbers
  • Your email and socials: so people can actually hire you

Save, then double-click index.html to see it in your browser. Repeat until you love it.

Photo tip: export work at 1600 px wide and shrink it for free at squoosh.app. Fast sites feel more premium.
3

Put it online, free

5 minutes
  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag your whole website folder onto the page
  3. You get a live link in seconds. Create a free account and claim the site so it stays online.
  4. In Site settings, change the name to yourname.netlify.app
Want your own domain? Buy yourname.com from any domain seller (usually under ₹1,000 a year) and connect it in Netlify's Domain management.
4

Add the premium feel

Optional · 10 minutes

The motion is already on. To add it anywhere else, copy an attribute and paste it inside any tag, like <h2 data-fx="reveal">.

Rising textWords rise into place as you scroll
data-fx="reveal"
Curtain revealA panel slides away to unveil a photo
data-fx="curtain"
Liquid glassFrosted card with light that follows the cursor
data-fx="glass"
TiltCards lean toward the cursor
data-fx="tilt"
Count upNumbers count up when seen
data-fx="count"
TickerAn endless scrolling line of words
data-fx="marquee"
Film grainSubtle texture over images
data-fx="grain"

All twelve effects are explained in the atelier-fx demo, our free open-source library.
