Good to know

Questions guests ask

Are there any fees on top of the rate?

No. Booking direct means no agency commission, no booking fee, no VAT and no hidden charges.

How does check-in work?

Check-in is self-service and remote, so you can arrive at any time. Rosario stays in touch by phone and email throughout your stay.

Is it suitable for working remotely?

Yes. The flat has free high-speed Wi-Fi, full air-conditioning and a quiet bedroom with a dressing table you can use as a desk.

Can I use the pool and gym?

Guests have full access to the building's amenities: the temperature-controlled pool, jacuzzi, gym, sauna, steam room, changing rooms, family room and children's area.

What's included in the kitchen?

Fridge, freezer, microwave, electric kettle, toaster, and a full set of cutlery, crockery, pans and bowls. An iron, ironing board and vacuum are also provided.
