01 · What we found

An honest look at flavoursofindiadubai.com

We reviewed the current homepage line by line. The restaurant has real strengths: three industry awards, professional dish photography, four experienced chefs and a loyal JBR following. The website hides almost all of it, and several problems are actively costing bookings and delivery orders.

Critical

Order links open restaurant dashboards

The Order menu points to restaurant-hub.deliveroo.net, talabat.portal.restaurant, restaurant.noon.partners and app.careemnow.com/merchant. A hungry customer hits a partner login and orders elsewhere.

FixPublic menu links for each platform, plus one “Order now” button that picks the right app on mobile.

Critical

Placeholder text is live

Lorem ipsum (“Praesent erat orci, consequat eu pellentesque…”, “Donec maximus ligula…”) sits on the homepage, next to template lines like “provide healthy and fresh food”.

FixFresh copy written around Awadhi and Mughlai heritage, the chefs and JBR dining.

Critical

The reservation form goes nowhere

Time slots stop at 10:30 PM and the WhatsApp button uses wa.me/0523981654, which is missing the UAE country code and fails to open a chat.

FixA booking form that drops a ready-to-send WhatsApp message, with an upgrade path to a table-booking system.

Critical

Stock template images of food you don’t serve

Nachos with guacamole, seafood pasta, a steak salad and dumplings appear in the hero, along with three blog posts by “Tanvir” showing fake “02 Comments”.

FixOnly your own dishes, interiors and films. Your existing shoot is strong enough to carry the site.

Major

Conflicting opening hours

The footer reads “Monday to Sunday 12AM to 12PM” and “Friday to Saturday 8.00am – 6.00pm”. Your shopfront sign says 12 PM to 12 AM.

FixOne source of truth for hours, matched to Google Business Profile.

Major

Typos and fake discounts on the menu

“Chef’s Special Bhuna Cost”, “Flavour Of India”. Every dish shows a struck-through higher price and random “20% Off” badges, which reads as untrustworthy.

FixA clean, correctly spelled menu with honest prices and a printable PDF/QR version.

Major

Dead pages and links

Twitter and Pinterest icons link to #; Portfolio, Services and FAQ are leftover template pages.

FixA focused site: Story, Menu, Chefs, Experience, Events, Visit.

Major

Invisible to Google and tourists

No restaurant schema, no menu markup, no Arabic, and a page heavy enough to fail Core Web Vitals on mobile.

FixRestaurant and menu structured data, fast mobile pages, optional Arabic version.

02 · Brand refresh

Keep the roundel. Add a royal second voice.

Your orange roundel is recognised on Deliveroo, Talabat and the shopfront, so we keep it for delivery and casual touchpoints. For the dining room, website and menus we add a refined wordmark, a copper-and-saffron palette and a heritage serif, so the brand can feel like a night out and a quick order at the same time.
