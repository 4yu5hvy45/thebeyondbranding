Our Story

A Culinary Journey from Lucknow to the Gulf

Flavours of India brings the court kitchens of Awadh and the roadside tandoors of the North to Jumeirah Beach Residence. Our chefs blend their own garam masala, cook the biryani in the dum style and give the nihari the slow hours it needs.

The result is food that tastes the way it does at home in India, served with the warmth that has made us a favourite with JBR families, hotel guests and the city’s Indian community.
