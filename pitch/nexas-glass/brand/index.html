01Idea

Glass is a light instrument.

Nexas sells what glass does to light: how it bends it, colours it, softens it and reveals it. The brand is built to show that effect, with warm light passing through real material, and without a founder on camera.

The mark · one stroke is glass

The X is where light passes through.

One stroke of the X is a pane of glass, carrying the dichroic gradient. Where the other stroke passes behind it, the line shifts sideways, just as a line does when you look at it through real glass. Every other letter is a quiet, hand-drawn monoline.

02Logo

A custom monoline wordmark.

The letters are drawn from scratch, not typeset: an even 6% stroke, a crossbar-free Λ, a geometric S, and the glass-blade X. Use the prism version wherever colour reproduces well. The outline version is for one-colour use such as etching, embossing and sandblasting.

03Construction & use

Drawn on a few simple rules.

  1. Stroke is 6% of cap height on every letter, with flat terminals.
  2. Λ has no crossbar and a flat apex the width of one stroke.
  3. Glass blade is 2.9× the stroke, filled with the prism gradient from amber at the base to mint at the top.
  4. Refraction: the stroke behind the glass shifts 1.7 stroke widths to the right and drops to 80% opacity.
  5. Tracking is 30% of cap height between letters. Never tighten it.

Clear space is half the cap height on every side.

Minimum size: wordmark 90 px or 22 mm wide. Below that, use the mark (16 px or 5 mm).

✕Don't condense or stretch it.

✕Don't recolour the prism.

✕Don't fill the glass solid on screen. Use the outline version.

✕Don't place it over busy imagery.

07Photography

Light through, never light on.

Low, warm, raking light passing through the glass. Deep shadows and stone and timber surfaces. One material per frame, and no people. Until Nexas has project photography, concept imagery like this is always captioned "Concept visual".

Do · backlit, warm, dark ground
Don't · flat, front-lit, grey
Do
  • Backlight or edge-light every glass shot
  • Golden-hour warmth, deep shadows
  • Stone, plaster and timber as context
  • Show the caustics and coloured light glass throws
  • Macro detail of texture and edges
Don't
  • Present concept or AI imagery as Nexas work
  • Flat front lighting or grey studio sweeps
  • Cool blue office stock photography
  • People as the subject
  • Filters that change the product's true colour
