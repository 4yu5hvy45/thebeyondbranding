How a project runs

From reference image to delivered glass.

Five steps, whether it's one feature wall or a full facade package.

01

Share the brief

Send drawings, a reference image or just a description, with the location and timeline.

02

Select & sample

We shortlist suitable glass and send samples or detailed photos where available.

03

Feasibility & quote

We check sizes, quantities and logistics, then send a clear quotation with lead times.

04

Fabrication & supply

Cutting, toughening, laminating or sourcing, coordinated to your schedule.

05

Delivery & support

Packed for transit, delivered to site, with one point of contact throughout.
