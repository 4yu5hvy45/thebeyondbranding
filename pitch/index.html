Beyond Branding pitch bar

Paste this before </body> on any speculative pitch site. The red bar below is a live preview.

<script src="https://thebeyondbranding.com/pitch/bar.js" defer
  data-client="Client name"
  data-offer="Save 25% on your website redesign"
  data-note="Limited projects each month · Enquire for details"></script>
AttributeWhat it does
data-clientShown as "Concept for …", and added to the WhatsApp message and tracking link.
data-offerMain line in the bar.
data-noteSmall line under the offer.
data-logosComma list from: marriott, radisson, dubai-media-office, esperti, soul-av, aiolec, wazirx, wemasy.
data-whatsappNumber for "Let's talk" (default +1 910 830 1588).
