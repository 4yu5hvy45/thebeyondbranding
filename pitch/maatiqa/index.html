We work with the oldest material there is, and a very new way of shaping it.

MAATIQA takes its name from maati ka, “of the earth”. Each piece starts as a digital form, is printed layer by layer in natural clay, then dried, refined and fired by hand.

The fine lines on every surface are the layers themselves. We leave them there on purpose.

Every piece begins as a single line.

One continuous line of clay, laid down layer upon layer. It is the oldest way to build a pot, guided here by a digital model and finished by hand.

  1. Prepare

    Clay is mixed and conditioned until it flows, yet holds its shape.

  2. Design

    Each form is drawn on screen: its profile, rhythm and twist.

  3. Print

    The printer coils the clay, one ring on top of the last.

  4. Finish

    Pieces dry slowly, are refined by hand and fired in a kiln.
