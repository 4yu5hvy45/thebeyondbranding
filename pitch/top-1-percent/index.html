Questions

Before you join.

Quick answers for students and parents.

Is this only for NEET students?

NEET is where we started and where most of our students are today. The mindset, study-strategy and mentorship programs help any student who wants to learn and grow better.

Is it online or offline?

Mentorship runs remotely across India, so students can join from any city and any schedule.

How is this different from coaching?

Coaching teaches the syllabus. Top 1 Percent adds what coaching leaves out: personal mentorship, accountability, study systems and the psychology of performing under pressure.

Can parents talk to the team first?

Yes. Book a free call and a mentor will walk you through the batches and answer your questions.
