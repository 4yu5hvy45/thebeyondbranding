    Guardrails.

    !
    Only show real workNo case studies or client logos for work we didn't do. One sharp-eyed London client is enough to lose the referral chain.
    !
    Use design references, don't copyUsing a brand's design .md file as reference is fine. Shipping its look is not. Rework the type, colour and layout until it doesn't read as their clone.
    ₹
    Sort the tax side nowFile a GST LUT for export services, read the FEMA freelancer rules, and find a CA before the bigger payments start.
    ₮
    Keep other people's money outDon't route Arjun's USDT cash-out, or anyone else's crypto, through our accounts.
    ⌂
    Get a presence that sticksGoogle often delists virtual offices. Set the Business Profile as a service-area business and get a few real reviews from AIOLEC and Soul AV.
    ÷
    Write the split before the workEvery shared project starts with one line: who leads it, the % split, who invoices. It only takes two minutes and avoids fights later.

    Beyond HQ, the app
    for founders who can't stop starting.

    Every ADHD founder has the same problem: lots of ideas, little follow-through, and tools built for neurotypical project managers. We're building the fix for ourselves first, and this page is v0.

    🎙️
    Voice in, tasks outDrop voice notes or a WhatsApp chat export. AI pulls out ideas, tasks and owners, the way this page came from our chat.
    🅿️
    Idea cooldownNew ideas are locked for 7 days. If it still excites you after that, it becomes a mission. It protects focus from novelty.
    ☝️
    One-thing screenThe app opens to a single task, not a dashboard. You get the full list only after you finish it.
    👥
    Built-in body doublingTap to join your partner's focus room. Seeing each other work keeps both of you going.
    🤖
    AI chaserIt nags clients and partners for you, writes the follow-ups and sends a short nightly recap. Nobody has to remember it.
    🔥
    Ship log, not streaksA visible wall of things you finished. Real wins give the dopamine, and a missed day costs nothing.
