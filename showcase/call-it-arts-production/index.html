How it works

From registration to wrap

Register

Bouncers, chefs and crew join the call list with their skills, city and availability.

Verify

Identity check through an authorised provider, two references and a screening call before anyone is deployed.

Call

You send a requirement. We match by city, role, night duty and budget, then confirm the call time.

Wrap

Duty log signed by your line producer, one GST invoice, and feedback recorded for next time.
