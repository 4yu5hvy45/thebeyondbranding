The idea

Nobody falls for a spec sheet. So we let buyers turn the product in their hand.

Aiolec makes commercial displays for places people see: shop windows, restaurants, receptions and high-footfall spaces. Three ranges cover indoor, high-brightness and double-sided use, from 32 to 75 inches.

The site is built like a launch: one idea per screen, real-world settings first, and an interactive 3D model of each display that buyers can rotate and resize. When they are ready, the Aiolec Advisor asks a few questions and points to the right model.
