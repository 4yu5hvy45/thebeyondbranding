Overview

In-store branding for a jewellery house, designed around the moment customers decide.

The Challenge

In jewellery, the store is part of the promise. The counter had to communicate heritage and craftsmanship as clearly as the pieces on display.

The Approach

Counter branding and in-store graphics in a refined palette with careful material choices—premium, without ever competing with the jewellery.

Delivered

Counter branding, in-store graphics and production artwork.

Result

A counter that communicates heritage as clearly as the pieces in the case.
