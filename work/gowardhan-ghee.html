Overview

Amazon listing design for Gowardhan Ghee, built to turn scrollers into buyers.

The Challenge

On Amazon, shoppers compare products in seconds with nothing but images to go on. The listing had to do the job of a salesperson.

The Approach

A listing image set that answers buyer questions in order—what it is, why it's better, how it's made, what's inside—with consistent brand styling throughout.

Delivered

Amazon listing image set and e-commerce creative.

Result

Listing images that answer buyer questions in the order they ask them.
