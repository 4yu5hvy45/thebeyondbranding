The idea

The numbers are the hero: potential, entry, target, stop loss.

Market Lens by Him publishes independent research and structured trade ideas on Indian equities, each with a defined entry, target and stop loss. Some calls are free; premium calls unlock after payment.

We designed it phone-first, because subscribers arrive from WhatsApp. Live calls lead, key numbers are large and readable, details stay locked until purchase, and closed calls become free research, so the track record is always public.
