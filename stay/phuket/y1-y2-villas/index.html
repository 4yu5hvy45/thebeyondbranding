01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Give the collection a name guests can remember.

The two properties already have strong guest ratings in the supplied research. The branding opportunity is to turn the internal unit codes into a considered villa collection while preserving the individuality of each stay.

This is a speculative design direction based only on the supplied lead research. It is not an approved property website. Final content, facilities and photography must be confirmed with the owner.

02 / PROPOSED SCOPE

One coherent guest experience.

Brand My Stay could include
  • Collection naming architecture for the villa pair
  • Shared identity system with Y1 and Y2 sub-identities
  • Brand guide, colours and typography
  • Guest printables and QR guide templates for both villas
  • Website with separate villa detail sections
  • Instagram launch kit and property storytelling
