A small morning ritual

The slow morning brew.

Never used a French press? Follow along. Good coffee doesn't need to be complicated.

STEP 01 / 06

Boil, then wait

Boil the kettle. Let the water rest for one minute before pouring. It should be hot, not aggressively bubbling.

04:00Your brew timer · four minutes
The timer is a guide for step five. Once the plunger is down, pour all the coffee out so it doesn't keep extracting. Adjust the amount of coffee to taste.
